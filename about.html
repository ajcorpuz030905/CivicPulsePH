ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Understanding Politics Through Technology

CivicPulse PH is an educational website that explores how technology, information, and responsible citizenship can help people understand political turbulence in the Philippines.

What Is CivicPulse PH?

CivicPulse PH is a student-oriented educational initiative designed to raise awareness about political turbulence and the role of digital technology in society. It encourages visitors to examine information critically, use credible sources, and participate in constructive civic discussions.

◎

Our Mission

To promote informed digital citizenship by helping people understand political issues, evaluate online information, and recognize the importance of responsible technology use.

◇

Our Vision

A society where people approach political information thoughtfully, respect different perspectives, and make decisions based on credible evidence.

✓

Our Goal

To provide accessible learning resources, technology solutions, and interactive activities that encourage critical thinking and civic awareness.

Why Focus on Political Turbulence?

Political turbulence can involve political uncertainty, conflict, polarization, and declining public trust. Misinformation and irresponsible online communication may intensify these challenges. Understanding these issues helps citizens recognize the importance of reliable information, accountability, and respectful public discussion.

SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND SOCIETY

How Does CivicPulse PH Connect to STS?

Science

Using evidence, research, and critical thinking to evaluate claims and understand social issues.

Technology

Examining how social media, digital platforms, and online tools affect the spread of political information.

Society

Understanding how technology influences public trust, political discussion, community relationships, and civic participation.

What Can Visitors Learn?

  • How to examine political claims before sharing them.
  • Why credible information matters during political uncertainty.
  • How technology can help or worsen misinformation.
  • Why media literacy and critical thinking are important.
  • How citizens can participate responsibly in society.

Think Critically. Share Responsibly. Participate Wisely.

Political differences are part of society. By combining critical thinking, responsible technology use, and respectful participation, people can help create a more informed public.
